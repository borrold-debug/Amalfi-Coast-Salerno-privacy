Privacy Policy
Amalfi Coast Salerno Guide

Amalfi Coast & Campania Travel Guide  ·  Effective date: June 2026

Amalfi Coast Salerno Guide App is a travel guide app for the Amalfi Coast, Salerno, Paestum, Capri, Naples and the Cilento region. This policy explains clearly and simply how we handle your privacy. The app does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

📋 1. Information We Collect

This is the simplest part: the app collects no personal data at all.

  • No location tracking: The app does not request or use your device location. The map shows points of interest only; it does not track where you are.
  • No analytics or tracking: We do not use any usage statistics, advertising IDs, or tracking tools.
  • No account required: The app does not require registration, login, or any personal information.

🔒 2. How the App Works

Because no data is collected, the app simply:

  • Displays interactive maps and points of interest
  • Provides travel content that works without an internet connection
  • Stores your preferences (language, favorites) only on your own device

✅ No advertising, no profiling, no commercial use of data of any kind.

🌐 3. Third-Party Services

Map data is based on OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors), bundled inside the app. No network requests are made to OpenStreetMap servers at runtime.

The app lets you open external websites. These are third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Maps – opened in your browser only when you tap "Open in Google Maps"
  • External transport links (Trenitalia, Busitalia, SITA Sud, Travelmar, Alicost) – external websites opened in your browser

When you open an external link, you are subject to the privacy policy of that service. We do not share any information with them.

📍 4. Permissions

The app requests no sensitive permissions — no location, no camera, no microphone, no contacts. The app works fully offline. An internet connection is only needed if you choose to open external links (Google Maps, transport websites).

👶 5. Children's Privacy

Amalfi Coast Salerno Guide is a family-friendly travel guide suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. The app contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.

🔄 6. Data Retention & Deletion

Since we do not collect personal data, there is nothing to delete. All app preferences (language, favorite sections) are stored locally on your device only. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data automatically.

⚖️ 7. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are a resident of the European Union, you have the right to:

  • Access any data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data
  • File a complaint with your national data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

📝 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically to reflect changes in the app or applicable law. We will notify users of significant changes by updating this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
